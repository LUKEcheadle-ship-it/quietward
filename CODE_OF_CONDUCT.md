# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Debate technical decisions without attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or pressure others to disclose security-sensitive details.

Security reports must follow `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that exposes credentials, private host data, exploit details, malware, or abusive material.

Project maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participation for unacceptable behavior. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
